Material Detail Review Board

Giza Plateau · Fourth Dynasty · c. 2560 BCE · peret season

Every swatch is drawn live in WebGL. Left half: the current flat material as rendered in the scene today. Right half: the proposed seeded procedural detail — pure GLSL hash noise (grain, sedimentary banding, compaction mottling, timber grain, water ripple) injected into the existing shared materials via onBeforeCompile. No textures, no network fetches, fully deterministic; frequencies here are representative, final values are object-space in scene.

Technique. One shared value-noise function (two-octave fBm over a sine hash), composed per material role from typed recipes. In the app these become MATERIAL_DETAIL_RECIPES in src/data/ (pure data, unit-tested) plus one onBeforeCompile injector in src/render/three/ with per-role customProgramCacheKey so programs never collide.
Cost. ALU-only: zero new textures, zero new draw calls, zero geometry. The Nile swatch is the only animated one — its phase derives from playback t, so scrubbing stays deterministic.
Sources. Material recipes follow the kept threejs-aaa-graphics-builder cookbook patterns; board direction follows frontend-design and the existing WonderForge token palette.
