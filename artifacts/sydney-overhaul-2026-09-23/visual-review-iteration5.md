## Highest-leverage remaining fixes

1. **Remove the portrait horizon seam and restore the intended atmosphere.** `production/mobile-0.92.png` and `production/mobile-1.png` show an abrupt straight boundary across the entire frame at approximately y=138; `.78` shows the same band lower in contrast. Fade or extend the actual distant terrain into atmospheric haze, then inspect the full late arc, not only t=1. Keep a readable blue upper sky during daytime. This is a direct acceptance blocker, not optional visual polish.

2. **Make the stable roof surface read as tiled, curved architecture.** Retain the corrected normals/shadow behavior. Add or recover low-frequency chevron panel fields that survive desktop and portrait filtering, and use directional lighting to reveal the shell surface curvature without recreating moire. Current `.78`, `.92` and `1` images prove the aliasing fix, but not the Spec 13 chevron-read requirement. Do not solve this with tiny extra triangles that disappear at the production camera.

3. **Finish the visible harbour fabric rather than increasing anonymous prop count.** Restore readable facade articulation on the current blank CBD masses; strengthen a few continuous terrace street fronts, garden canopy groups and road endpoints. The new varied north-shore structures are an improvement, but vast flat empty plots and bare towers still read as a world blockout (`production/desktop-0.58.png`, `production/desktop-1.png`). This does not require a fully modeled city: it requires a coherent near/middle distance at the fixed film camera.
