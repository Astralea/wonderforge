# Spec 29 — Eiffel second-floor relay integration boundary

The first-floor long-load renderer remains the sole owner of `summit-access-stair-m000-c000` after its existing 280-second chapter. A later relay may move that same carrier, payload, retained sling, master link, cart and visible rope by supplying an explicit `EiffelSecondFloorRelaySample`. The hook never loads a tower kit or another payload asset.

Every relay sample keeps `ownsPayload:true` and `seated:false`. It distinguishes carrier support (`cart`, `tackle`, or `upper-receiver`) from cart support, cart attachment, sling support and the four-fastener state. The payload pose is an explicit witness derived from the preserved carrier-local transform. Final seating is outside this hook and requires a later ownership transfer.

The default renderer update takes no relay sample and preserves the shipped 280-second film exactly. No relay duration, route, hardware asset or opaque transition is introduced before the physical route is authored and checked. The known first-floor endpoint must be the first sample of any future relay; ground provenance and later summit installation remain separate gates.

The isolated route clock begins only after the separate connector-and-bolt handoff. It lifts the carrier from `[-8.5,58.2800024414,-4]` to `[-8.5,117.70,-4]` over 106 seconds, traverses the full 6.862215 m diagonal to `[-15,117.70,-1.8]` over 16 seconds, and lowers to the receiving cart at `[-15,116.47999939,-1.8]` over four seconds. The original first-floor rope stays visible at its terminal state. A separate 8 mm second-hoist rope follows the raised guide and moving sheave. Its source drive, winding and reaction path remain unadmitted.
