Pass 6 filled each tall bay with two face-spanning X-pairs; at 520–780 m those
diagonals were ~14° and read as stacked beams. Pass 7 splits wide faces into
**two columns of roughly square X-bays** (0.4 m bars), and builds the shaft as
**one on-axis needle** instead of four overlapping pylons. Champ allées sit
closer to the feet; Haussmann roofs are a street grid plus infill (420 trees /
520 roofs).

Dawn shows four piers, creeper cranes, and the first X-bays. Mid-BUILD shows
leaning lattice legs and a first-platform belt. Night shows four legs joining
a lanterned shaft with sky through the diamonds. This is **not** a Giza-density
accept: members are still coarse at hold distance, and K3 has not signed off.
