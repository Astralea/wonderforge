synthesizer, electric guitar, trailer percussion, or hammer-and-anvil beat.

Morning on the Champ de Mars. Begin with spacious high strings and woodwind conversation. Over three minutes unfold several contrasting lyrical phrases, with a very gentle walking pulse arriving halfway through. End quietly, open and unresolved, ready for the next chapter.
