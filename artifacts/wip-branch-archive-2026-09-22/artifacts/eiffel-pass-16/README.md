Pass 16 replaces pass 15’s 3×3 diagonal lace with an **axis-aligned 4×3
mullion grid** on outward faces wider than 6 m. Rotated diagonals inside
the panel were sheared into splinters by non-uniform instance scale.
Face X-braces stay as authored `brace` parts. Inner walls and lantern
faces stay without lace.

Dawn shows piers and creeper labour. Mid-BUILD can show four leaning
pylons with a first-platform belt and a shaft climber. Night shows four
legs into a lanterned needle. This is **not** a Giza-density accept: at
520–780 m the monument still reads as an open truss rather than Giza-scale
lace, and K3 has not signed off.
