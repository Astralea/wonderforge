# Summit operation integration boundary

`sampleEiffelSummitOperation` is a candidate mechanism clock only. Its 319 seconds are c001 cargo (0–112), the supported gin-pole climb (112–182), empty-tackle return (182–207), and c002 cargo (207–319). The second stock arrival and attachment remain omitted and explicitly unadmitted. The m073 cargo drive remains fixed; during the climb the empty cargo block stays at a constant pose relative to the rising head and its rope pays out the extra riser length. c001 and children m074/m075 remain seated from 112 seconds onward.

Production cannot yet dispatch this clock. `eiffelFilm.ts` currently ends the real long-load chain with an opaque omission after the first-floor crossing and explicitly says the second-floor, 197 m relay and final installation are not shown. `eiffelProductionConstruction.ts` still owns the summit operation endpoints and does not provide a demonstrated path from those relays to the terrace cart at `[-6.4701368053,280.5899952,0]`. The new sampler therefore exposes `groundToTerraceAdmitted:false`; a film insertion must start only after a separate identity-preserving relay reaches that cart.

The smallest eventual adapter is one insertion after that relay: render c001 cargo, retain its three exact IDs seated, render the 70-second climb with the cargo tackle parked and both old/new guide visibility driven by the operation sample, then render c002 cargo. The renderer must keep the fixed m073 drive visible in all phases and must never show c002 or its new supports before c001 seats. Camera and film seconds should be derived only after the operation GLB, worker access, and rope/source clearance gates pass.
