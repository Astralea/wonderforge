I’ve been building WonderForge: four short 3D films of Giza, Stonehenge, the Colosseum and the Eiffel Tower taking shape.

You can scrub through construction and turn narration on. Best on desktop.

https://wonderforge.pages.dev/

## Suggested accompanying video

Use about 20–30 seconds of actual film footage. Open directly on one readable
construction movement, show a second site briefly, then finish with Stonehenge's
sun or the Colosseum's moonrise. Preserve an uninterrupted few seconds of the
ending. Include the project name briefly at the end; do not replace the actual
experience with a logo-only trailer. Captions should make the clip understandable
with sound off. These are editorial recommendations, not platform constraints.

Post only after the tested local build is deployed and checked at the public
URL. Current public deployment still serves the older main-DJwnCTGh.js bundle.
The repository can remain private. Visitors can still download the JavaScript,
models and audio that their browser needs to run the site.

A custom domain can wait. The immediate useful feedback is whether a first-time
visitor can enter a film, understand one construction operation, and reach the
ending without a loading or audio problem. A quick real-phone visit through
Twitter/X is the remaining device check; automated mobile viewports do not
establish that.
