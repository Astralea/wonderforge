# Colosseum chapter evidence check

2026-09-20. Supplement to root's seven-chapter rewrite. Official archaeology/museum sources only; no changes to Colosseum application data made by this reviewer. The current seven sentences are supported at their stated level, with construction staging and the astronomical ending kept distinct from historical claims.

| Chapter | Supporting primary/official source | What it supports and the limit |
|---|---|---|
| Nero's former lake | [Parco archeologico del Colosseo: Walking in Nero's footsteps](https://colosseo.it/en/itineraries/walking-in-the-parco-in-neros-footsteps/) | The artificial lake was removed and the Flavian amphitheatre replaced it. The film opens after drainage: the caption can identify the former site without showing a surviving lake. |
| Travertine from Tivoli | [Soprintendenza fieldwork report, Tivoli, by Zaccaria Mari](https://www.sabap-rm-met.beniculturali.it/it/430/scavi-ricerche-valorizzazione-e-musealizzazione/1810/tivoli), together with [the Park's southern-ambulatories construction account](https://colosseo.it/en/southern-ambulatories/) | The fieldwork report identifies the Barco quarry as a source for the Colosseum. The Park explains travertine bases and piers supporting the vaults. Together they support “load-bearing piers … travertine quarried near Tivoli.” No precise haul distance or disputed tonnage is needed. |
| Lifting the blocks | [Vatican Museums: Mausoleum of the Haterii](https://www.museivaticani.va/content/museivaticani/en/collezioni/musei/museo-gregoriano-profano/Mausoleo-degli-Haterii.html) | The relief represents a large wheel-powered lifting machine, and the builders' memorial includes Flavian projects. It dates from the early second century. It supports the technological reconstruction, not proof that every modeled crane/rig stood at this exact position in AD 80. Retain “In this reconstruction.” |
| Under the vaults | [Park: The Construction System of the Colosseum](https://colosseo.it/en/southern-ambulatories/) | Excavated bases, piers, foundations and vaulted corridors are direct architectural evidence. This page does not independently establish every temporary timber support in the model. The sentence's “Here” correctly refers to the film's timber-centering interpretation. Do not cite this source as a surviving full timber erection manual. |
| A place in the crowd | [Park: The Cavea and the Belvedere Terrace](https://colosseo.it/en/marvels/the-cavea-of-the-colosseum-and-the-belvedere-terrace/) | The official account explicitly describes hierarchical seating by social class. “Roman social rank” is a concise supported paraphrase, without pretending all spectators had equal access. |
| Titus opens the arena | [Park epigraphy lesson: fifth-century earthquakes and the end of games](https://colosseo.it/en/event/stories-from-the-colosseum-epigraphy-lessons-the-earthquakes-of-the-5th-century-and-the-end-of-the-games/) | This account identifies the original inaugural inscription as Titus's, AD 80. It provides a stronger archaeological anchor than a generic tourism summary. [The Park's Blood and the Arena account](https://colosseo.it/en/event/blood-and-the-arena/) separately connects Titus's AD 80 games with the amphitheatre's opening. “Inaugurates” is preferable to claiming every later addition was already complete. |
| Moonrise over Rome | Current production astronomy sample and actual final shot, owned/verified by root and celestial agent | “After sunset, a nearly full Moon rises over Rome” is an authored-film observation. It is not evidence of an inauguration-night sky or a known festival date. Preserve that separation; the astronomical date must not quietly become an archaeological claim. |
