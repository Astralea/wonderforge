# Eiffel upper material chain — draft

## Scene lifetime during local verification

An Eiffel scene disposed while its Paris GLB is loading must discard that parsed
source before facade analysis and spatial batching. Release its unique geometry,
materials, textures and image bitmaps once; it must not mount a cancelled city or
change the geometry/materials of a scene that remains active. This handles scene
navigation as well as repeated offline scene construction without extending test
timeouts or weakening the existing geometry checks.

The shipped film ends its visible material chain with the same `summit-access-stair-m000-c000` and retained 6.317500305 m carrier landed on the second-floor receiving cart. The exact relay endpoint is carrier `[-15,116.47999938964844,-1.8]`, master `[-15,123.36249969482422,-1.8]`, with cart origin `[-15,116.13999938964844,-1.8]`. Arrival is not final installation. The subsequent 197 m transfer, carrier extraction and final tower seating remain required work; this specification does not claim that they exist already.

## First bounded continuation: secure the upper cart, then release the second hoist

Keep the current payload/carrier/master identity and pose throughout this stationary receiving operation. Retain the first-floor cart, worker, four removed fasteners, tools and first hoist at their existing terminal states. Four **new** upper-cart fastener identities (`upper-cart-bolt-0` through `upper-cart-bolt-3`) begin supported on a separately authored upper tray. They are never aliases for the old `cart-bolt-*` objects. A distinct upper receiving worker and wrench remain present from the prepared state.

Each new fastener must be picked up, carried with a matching hand, aligned above its actual upper-cart hole, inserted vertically and tightened through four complete turns. A hand, tray or engaged cart nut must own every fastener throughout. No lateral motion is allowed after the shaft enters the shoe. The carrier stays on the upper-cart bed and the hoist stays connected until all four fasteners are fully inserted and tightened. The worker then walks to actual supported access, climbs, transfers the retained master onto its existing carrier saddle by unloading the second connector, opens the keeper, withdraws the pin and leaves the released connector with its own rope. Opening the keeper or pin while the upper cart is unsecured is invalid. No source geometry is scaled and no worker or fastener teleports.

## Verification and admission limits

Tests must establish deterministic forward/reverse sampling, all phase-join continuity, one planted foot during walking, connected fixed-length worker IK, bolt holder/hand contact, vertical insertion, four-turn tightening, unique old/new identities, invariant landed payload/carrier poses and the ordering of secure/unload/keeper/pin events. Actual exported cart holes, bolt/tray contacts, supported access, posed worker/solid clearance and rope/connector geometry require separate source and renderer checks before production integration. Reusing an old trajectory does not inherit clearance at a new height/location.

The complete upper chain additionally requires a supported 197 m receiver and load route, causal equipment arrival/erection, continuing material identity, extraction from the carrier, final assembly fastening, and removal or retained support of every temporary sling/fixture. These remain explicit future stages. This draft offers no strength, torque, rope inventory, historic reconstruction or whole-chain physical certification.
