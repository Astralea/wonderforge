# First-floor hoist drive

The bounded long-load chapter must show a source of mechanical motion beside
its existing drum. A compact, interpreted steam winch uses a boiler, connected
steam pipe, horizontal cylinder, sliding piston rod, fixed-length connecting
rod, crank, flywheel and a 2:1 12-tooth pinion engaging the existing 24-tooth drum gear.
Its angle is derived from the same hoist rope payout as the drum; no independent
idle loop may continue to drive a stationary load. An operator stands on the
existing first-floor deck and holds the steam control. All solid parts retain
their dimensions throughout playback and reverse seeks.
