## Historical and visual scope

An interpretive1406–1420 founding-period construction story, centered on the
three principal axial halls, terraces, gate, side courts and an inhabited Beijing
setting. Surviving palace photographs establish a recognizable architectural
vocabulary; current Taihe Hall's1695–1697 rebuilding is not claimed to be exact
1420 fabric. Dimensions and hidden elevations inferred by the modeler must be
labeled in the manifest and production notes. Do not import modern signage,
Qing furnishings, modern vehicles or unsupported exact labor totals.

Build outward from original Blender geometry: open red timber colonnades,
white stone terraces, fitted beams and bracket clusters, curved layered eaves,
gold glazed roof fields, ridge ornaments and dark lattice infill. Principal,
central and rear hall roofs have distinct hipped, pyramidal and hip-and-gable
silhouettes. Surrounding courts, gates, walls, gray-roof dwellings, material
yards, traffic and the moat create depth. No repeated generic cone roofs or
solid hall box hiding columns. Geometry is photo/plan-guided interpretation,
not photogrammetry. Model a full composition, not a lone pavilion in empty land.

## Film and physical thesis

180 seconds. Continuous camera rotation from the opening, with eased approaches
to three representative actions and a wide return after each. Keep the world
visible; floating captions and ElevenLabs narration replace fullscreen cards.

-0–18s: occupied approach, axis and prepared ground; arriving materials.
-18–50s: one identified facing stone travels on a carrier, aligns and seats on
 an existing foundation support. Other foundation work proceeds in parallel.
-50–98s: one full-size timber column travels horizontally on a supported cart,
 reaches its stone base, pivots under a restrained timber raising rig and is
 braced. Return to the other completed column/beam bays.
-98–150s: a small roof-material delivery reaches an existing scaffold; a worker
 fits a tile/member to supported framing. No entire prefabricated golden roof
 suspended in the air. Repetitive work progresses in bounded parallel lanes.
-150–180s: broaden across completed courts and retain an eight-second reveal.

These are plausible authored operations, not a claim to recover a documented
1420 work order. Featured pieces retain identity, dimensions, continuous poses
and visible support from source to seat. Completed solids stay fixed. Never
scale solids or scaffolds, reveal material under the ground, or hide a missing
material path with a camera obstruction. Workers have real jobs, distinct gait,
reachable hand contacts, supported feet and a climb before elevated work.
Time compression may omit repeated work, as requested by the owner; it does
not justify a whole visible building suddenly appearing. No exhaustive new
micro-operation chapter for every tile or temporary fitting.
